Make Your Story Person

This is a working prototype we’re trialling with families. It helps you and your child make a recognisable story character together, then use that same person across personalised stories and resources.

Photos stay with you. You don’t upload photos to this page. When you reach ChatGPT, attach the photo(s) there yourself. Whiz Kids only receives what you later choose to email us.
