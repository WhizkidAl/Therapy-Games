Suggested subject

Action requested by [DATE] — [CHILD]'s therapy resources for PAT

Message

Hi everyone, We're setting up [CHILD]'s Portable Therapy Access (PAT) START page so practice activities and familiar supports are easy to find, and can be used more consistently across home, school and therapy. The simplest way to contribute is to send me ONE working link to a resource you already maintain (for example, a Google Doc, OneDrive/SharePoint folder, Padlet or other suitable page). Please include a short note telling me what to practise, how often, what help is needed and when the task is complete. You can keep updating that resource at the same link without needing us to change the START button. Alternatively, just email me your current PDF, Word document or activity sheet. I'll add a permitted copy to our restricted shared resources. There's no need to rebuild your work, transfer your intellectual property or move onto another platform. Our calendar has regular practice reminders. Most of those times are for [CHILD] to choose an activity from START. If you'd like a specific target practised at a particular time (for example, a speech-sound target), please let me know the activity, suggested duration and frequency; I'll confirm which practice window fits. Please continue sending actual appointment invitations to my usual email address AND invite [CHILD]'s dedicated calendar address [CHILD CALENDAR EMAIL] so bookings are visible on the tablet. These are separate from practice reminders. We're starting with the family's approved visual schedules and phonics supports in [CHILD]'s portable pack. If you recommend a different visual or material, send me the link or file and a brief explanation so we can keep everyday supports consistent across settings. Please send your link/materials by [DATE], or reply by that date if something prevents you from doing so. Let me know what help you need and when you expect to have the material ready. Please check with me before undertaking any additional billable setup or licensing. The short provider guide is here: https://whizkidal.github.io/Therapy-Games/provider-resources/provider-guide.html Thanks so much for helping us make this practical and manageable. [PARENT NAME]

Before sending

  1. Fill in the child, parent, two calendar email addresses and due date in the private email.
  2. Supply any private family folder invitation separately; avoid putting restricted link details on public pages.
  3. Attach the family-specific practice schedule if providers need exact days/times; do not copy identifiable clinical information into this public template.
