About this tool and recordings

Thirty pictured CVC words, with single-letter sound–spelling correspondences. Use the activities your speech pathologist has demonstrated. Checked responses are informal practice information, not a clinical assessment. They do not establish how long someone practised.

Recordings and practice responses stay in this browser. They are not emailed or uploaded by this tool. These recordings are separate from recordings in a downloaded START file. If the microphone is blocked, use Import audio. Keep a recording backup before clearing browser data.
