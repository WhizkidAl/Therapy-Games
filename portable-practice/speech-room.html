For adults

Open Speech from My Base → My practice → My therapy and school activities. My Base saves its settings, feedback and coins to the family’s private cloud workspace. These speech tools open separately and do not automatically send recordings, completed activities or coin claims back to My Base. Return to My Base to record how it went; outside activities need parent coin approval. Recordings made inside a speech tool stay in that browser’s tool storage unless exported.
