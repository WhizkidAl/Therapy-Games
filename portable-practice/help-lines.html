For Dad and the therapy team

This is supported practice, not a right-or-wrong quiz. Discuss the reasons behind a response, model the strategy and let him try it. A truthful “not yet”, “I don’t remember” or “I’d rather not share” is a valid reflection. Do not require disclosure of upsetting experiences.

Agree the task before beginning. A useful session includes the story, a practised response, a different situation and reflection. The My Base plan controls the duration or clinician-set finish criteria; completing this page alone does not earn a coin.

Return to My Base → Finished or need help to record the session. Dad checks the actual work and the weekly limits. Extra practice does not earn unlimited coins.
