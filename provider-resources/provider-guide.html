Portable Therapy Access / provider guide

Make your therapy resources easy to find and use

The family's PAT START page is a doorway to activities, shared visual supports and practice choices. You can keep your own resources where they are and update them whenever needed.

The minimum request: Send the parent one working link or an existing document, plus a short note on frequency, adult support and whether it is a choice or a requested practice task. No new platform is required.

1 · Roles and boundaries

Provider

Selects the clinical goal, activities, teaching cues, dosage recommendations and signs that a task is complete; maintains their own resource.

Parent / family

Controls who can access resources, which START card opens them, the weekly practice timetable and whether a nominated window is allocated to a particular task.

Child

Uses activities that are accessible at their current skill level, with appropriate adult support. A reminder is a prompt, not proof that an activity happened.

Shared responsibility

Keep familiar visuals and wording consistent, make adjustments collaboratively and avoid multiple disconnected sets of routine resources.

2 · Two easy ways to contribute

Option A — stable live link (preferred)Option B — send an existing file
Share a viewable document, folder, Padlet or page you already manage. Keep updating the contents at the same address; the family's START button can stay unchanged. Send a permitted PDF, Word document or image to the parent. The family can add it to their restricted resource choices. Send a replacement when you revise it.

You retain control and intellectual property in your therapy materials. You are not being asked to hand over master templates, editable clinical plans or ownership of your work.

3 · Practice reminders are not therapy bookings

The family sets recurring practice windows in the child's calendar. Once the family has imported them and notifications are enabled on the device, an alert sounds; the child can then open START and choose from the available activities. The family can move, pause or remove any window.

Ordinary practice — choice

Alarm → START → choose an activity → practise with support. Most links are simply one of the child's available choices. A reminder does not reserve that window for any provider.

Specific target — by agreement

If an intervention requires a named task or dosage, ask the parent to allocate a particular practice window. Provide the exact target, time required, cues and what the adult should do. The parent confirms or proposes another time.

Example: A Cycles speech-sound program may call for two short practices of the current target during the week. The speech pathologist sends the current word list, modelling/cueing instructions and suggested duration; the parent chooses which suitable windows, if any, to allocate.

Actual therapy appointments are different. Continue sending normal booking invitations to the parent's established contact address and also invite the child's designated calendar account, using the private addresses supplied by the parent. Include service name, location/link and an agreed visual symbol alongside text. Do not publish account addresses here.

4 · What to send with your resource

  1. One full link beginning with https://, or an existing PDF/DOCX/image. The link must work for the intended viewer; don't send a local file path, password or temporary editing link.
  2. Service and activity: which START card or area it belongs under, and the goal or task in plain language.
  3. Practice recommendation: suggested times per week, minutes per attempt and whether it is a CHOICE or REQUESTED TIME (subject to parent confirmation).
  4. Teaching support: modelling, prompts, cues or adult assistance needed, and what 'finished' looks like.
  5. Any change to routine resources: what physical pack item or visual you want used and why.

Updating an existing file at the same location should not require changing START. If you move the resource to a new address, email the parent its new full link and the name of the START card to update.

5 · Physical pack and shared visual supports

Keep the familiar phonics materials, visual schedules and other supports available in the child's portable pack across home, school and therapy. Where the family has approved a shared visual set, use it as a starting point instead of creating duplicate systems.

Providers can recommend different visuals or materials. Please explain what the alternative achieves and give the parent a link or print-ready copy. The family coordinates whether to change the everyday tablet cards and physical resources; providers can still use other clinically appropriate materials in their own sessions.

Useful formats: a stable live https:// link; a viewable Google/Microsoft document; or PDF/Word for written tasks. For a new printable image, send a clear PDF or PNG/JPG sized for the intended print format.

Confidentiality and permissions: Keep child-specific records, identifiable documents, personalised schedules and links requiring confidentiality in a restricted family/provider location, not in public GitHub. Only share content you have authority to distribute. Agree any additional billable preparation or licensing with the family before doing it.

6 · Action and confirmation

Respond by the date in the family's invitation with the live link or existing materials and a brief activity note. If you cannot complete it by then, reply by the requested date with the reason, any support required and an expected completion date.

That's it. You continue to maintain your own clinical activities; the family maintains one consistent doorway to them.
